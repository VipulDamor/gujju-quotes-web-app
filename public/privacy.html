1. Introduction

Welcome to Gujju Quotes App. We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This privacy policy applies to all information collected through our Android application and related digital services.

By using our service, you agree to the collection and use of information in relation to this policy.

2. Information Collection & Use

For a better experience while using our Service, we may require you to provide us with certain personally identifiable information. The information that we request will be retained by us and used as described in this privacy policy.

The app does use third party services that may collect information used to identify you.

3. Third-Party Services

We rely on industry-standard providers to power our app features. Each has their own privacy practices:

Core infrastructure and account management.

AdMob

Firebase Analytics

Firebase Crashlytics

4. App Permissions & Data

To provide specific features like the "Status Downloader", we may request access to your device's media folders. We want to emphasize:

  • Media Access: Used only to browse WhatsApp status folders. We do not upload your personal photos.
  • Google Drive: Used for voluntary backups. We only access the "App Data" folder we create.
  • Notifications: Used for daily inspiration and system alerts.

5. Payments & In-App Purchases

Security is our priority. All financial transactions are handled via Google Play Billing. We never see or store your payment card details, bank accounts, or billing address. All payment processing is subject to Google Play's own terms and conditions.
