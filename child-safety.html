Our Safety Framework

Gujju Quotes App is built to inspire. We believe that inspiration cannot exist without a foundation of absolute safety for children and vulnerable individuals.

Prohibited Material

We strictly prohibit the creation, storage, or distribution of Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM). Any content that depicts child sexual abuse or exploits children is fundamentally forbidden on our platform.

Grooming & Predatory Behavior

Predatory behavior, including attempts to "groom" or establish inappropriate contact with minors, is detected through advanced monitoring and leads to immediate permanent exclusion.

Enforcement Strategy

Our enforcement team operates 24/7. Upon detecting or receiving a report of a safety violation, we take action within a targeted timeframe of less than 60 minutes.
